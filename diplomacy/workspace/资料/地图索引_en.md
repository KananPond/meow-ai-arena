# Province index and adjacency

English names and three-letter codes follow the referee's standard map. A = army, F = fleet; NC/SC/EC = north/south/east coast.
WATER = sea, COAST = coastal province, LAND = inland province. These lists describe direct moves; army convoys follow the full rules separately.
Support legality is determined by the current phase's legal_orders; geographic adjacency does not necessarily mean a fleet can reach a province.
