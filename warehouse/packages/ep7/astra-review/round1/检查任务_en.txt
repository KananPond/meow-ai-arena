You are the independent regression examiner for this temporary test. Inspect all 12 submitted warehouse scheduling C++ programs under `targets/` and find and verify real defects wherever possible. No exemptions: Pangu, Dots, Wenxin, LongCat, Qwen27B, Mistral, Hunyuan, Step, Mercury, Haiku, Sonnet, Opus. `提交清单.json` records their hashes.

`baseline/` is the sole behavioral authority; `SPEC.md` and `原始主程任务.md` provide context. Preserve baseline quirks; unintuitive behavior is not itself a defect. Read code and handoffs first, then construct targeted counterexamples and necessary differential tests. Allocate attention fairly rather than stopping after random public-workload testing.

Work only here. `targets/` and `baseline/` are read-only; do not modify or repair submissions. Put builds and temporary files in `build/` and `work/`. Do not access the organizer's event folders, other competition folders, or other model sessions; no network, other AI, or subagents. Local compilation, testing, and analysis are allowed. Compile all top-level `src/*.cpp` with `g++ -O2 -std=c++20 -static`. `tools/measure.py` and `check_input.py` are available. Other programs run here: terminate only your own processes by PID, never in bulk by name.

Use the original verdict rules: pass `tools/check_input.py` without `--full`; baseline finishes normally within 10 seconds; a differing output (only CRLF normalized), abnormal exit, compilation failure, or runtime over 60 seconds constitutes a valid counterexample. First allow 100 seconds; if completed in 60–100 seconds, repeat twice with 60-second limits and uphold only if all three exceed 60 seconds. Illegal inputs are invalid; inputs making both programs slow do not qualify. Findings requiring the larger legal `--full` range must be listed separately as out-of-scope additions.

For each submission retain up to three actually reproduced valid counterexamples with different root causes under `findings/<目标>/`, including `.in`, baseline/target outputs or diffs, commands, exit codes, and timings. Explore more candidates if useful, but do not fill a quota. Explain root causes and triggers; multiple inputs for one fault are not multiple defects. Write “not found in this inspection” when appropriate, never a proof of defect-free code.

Write `report.md` covering each submission's inspection coverage, verified defects, unverified suspicions, and evidence paths, plus `summary.json` for aggregation. This is temporary diagnosis, with no official score or maintainability review. Finish with a brief account of all 12.

Organizer supplement, effective this round: official performance timing is running on this machine. Search only for correctness defects such as output disagreement, crashes, and invalid output. Do not perform performance, runtime, or timeout tests or construct long-running large inputs. Run at most 2 tested programs concurrently; abandon any input whose program exceeds 60 seconds and use a smaller one. Timeouts do not count as findings this round.
