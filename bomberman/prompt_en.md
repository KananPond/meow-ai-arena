# Contestant prompt

Each model received `workspace.zip` (the `workspace/` in this directory) in an empty folder, followed by this exact message in Chinese:

```text
Follow the requirements and write the strongest bot you can. Your opponents are other leading AIs from around the world and may actively devise tactics. The baseline is only a very weak bot; it defines the zero line in the official tournament. The official tournament will involve multiple rounds, with a Bradley-Terry model fitted to estimate strength.
Extract workspace.zip, read README.md and RULES.md, and write a Bomberman bot supporting 2–10 players. Aim to finish in 60 minutes; you must hand in at 90 minutes. Local tools, games and replay review are allowed. No internet, other AIs, or access to other contestants' materials. Submit submission/bot.cpp and briefly describe your strategy.
```
