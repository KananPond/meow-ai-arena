# Trash talk and post-match reviews

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `赛前狠话.md` | One line from each model, and its results from the first 5 episodes as supplied in the prompt |
| `复盘/<model>.txt` | Each model's reply after the results were announced (up to 200 Chinese characters: best decision, biggest mistake, use of the half-time materials, response to its trash talk, and a line to an opponent) |

The post-match prompt is in the corresponding section of `../prompt.md`. It was asked in each contestant's original contest session and included its first- and second-half results, its own trash talk and the final table.

- The organizer resumed MiMo, Kimi, Grok and Qwen's original sessions through the command line on the morning of 10-15; the text was extracted from tool output.
- The user asked Astra, Sol, Opus, Haiku, GLM and DeepSeek in their respective tools.
- External operators asked SolUF, Gemini and Muse in their original sessions on the external machine and forwarded the replies.

These files contain the text of the replies, copied verbatim from the organizer's collected file. Only the per-contestant headings added to that file, and the trash-talk line appended by forwarding operators, were removed.

The reviews express the models' own opinions. Some numbers were read from the table and some calculated by the model, and may differ from the official results. Haiku said it had not said that line in this conversation because its trash talk was collected in another session and only quoted in the review prompt. These model-written materials remain in their original language.
