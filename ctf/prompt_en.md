# Contestant prompt

Each model received `workspace.zip` (this directory's `workspace/`, with only a compiled baseline at the time) in an empty folder, followed by the exact Chinese message below. The organizer added no further hints, only neutral resumption or time-credit messages after interruptions (see [CONDITIONS_en.md](CONDITIONS_en.md)).

```text
Follow the requirements and write the strongest bot you can. Your opponents are other leading AIs from around the world and may actively devise tactics. The baseline is a medium-strength bot with a conventional approach; it defines the zero line in the official tournament. Beating it does not mean you are strong enough: the official opponents are much stronger. The official tournament will involve multiple rounds, with a Bradley-Terry model fitted to estimate strength.
Extract workspace.zip, read README.md and RULES.md, and write a Capture the Flag bot supporting 2–15 sides. You have 60 minutes. Only the final bot's strength counts, with no bonus for handing in early, so use the time fully to keep improving. Some overrun is allowed, but hand-in is forced at 90 minutes. Local tools, games and replay review are allowed. No internet, other AIs, or access to other contestants' materials. Submit workspace/submission/bot.cpp and briefly describe your strategy.
```
