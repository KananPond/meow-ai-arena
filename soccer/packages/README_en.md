# Team-event instructions

[format.md](format.md) is the unchanged template prepended to the base contestant-package README. Braced fields were filled with slots, teammates and timings: six teams, a 5-minute meeting, 40 minutes per half, 32/44 seeds at half time/final.

The pre-event template describes team scoring only. Individual teams and 50%/50% weighting were added through the [half-time message](../prompt_en.md) at the start of the second half. Its no-substantial-copying rule supersedes the older base-rule wording.

`clock.py` and `tc.py` depend on organizer services not included here. This is not a fully runnable historical development workspace. See the [event guide](../README_en.md) and [schedules](../tournament/README_en.md) for half-time material and reconstruction.
